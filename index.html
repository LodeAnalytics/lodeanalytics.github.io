How your data gets from scattered to trusted

Each layer has one job, so problems are easy to find and every report is built from the same checked data. Everything runs in your own Microsoft tenant, on a schedule.

  1. Connect your sources

    ERP, CRM, SaaS apps, and SQL databases, including on-premises systems.

  2. Ingest on a schedule

    Incremental loads pull only new or changed records, with retries and run logging.

  3. Land in your lakehouse

    One governed store in OneLake, as open Delta tables you own.

  4. Refine through medallion layers

    Raw history is kept, then cleaned and conformed, then shaped with your business rules.

    BronzeSilverGold

  5. Model in the warehouse

    Curated tables and views with SQL access for analysts and other tools.

  6. Report in Power BI

    Shared measures, row-level security where needed, and dashboards that refresh on their own.
